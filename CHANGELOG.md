# What's new in PureClean

## 1.0.0 — On the Mac App Store

- **PureMac is now PureClean.** Same app, new name.
- PureClean is now on the **Mac App Store**, with one yearly subscription that includes every
  feature, all updates and support. New subscribers get the **first week free**. Builds you make from this source code stay free.
- Runs in Apple's **App Sandbox**: PureClean sees only the folders you allow — your home folder, and
  Macintosh HD the first time you scan the entire disk.
- Apps you're using are never quit by PureClean: **Show** brings them to the front so you can quit
  them, and their caches join the cleanup by themselves.
- **Performance** now shows the whole Mac's CPU use; simulators are counted from their files.
- The local database no longer keeps a large log file after big scans.
- Available in 23 languages, now on the App Store in every one of them.

## 0.13.0 — First public source release

PureClean's source code is now public. This version brings the biggest Smart Clean update yet.

### Smart Clean: more space, fewer decisions
- Finds about **twice as much** to free, still choosing only what your Mac rebuilds by itself: web
  caches of desktop apps such as code editors and chat apps, downloaded app updates, developer tool
  caches, and logs and crash reports untouched for a month.
- Opens ready: one total and one **Review & Clean** button.
- **Free up more:** one tap each for unused developer data, installers older than a month and
  leftovers of deleted apps — plus how much is already waiting in your Trash.
- **Apps you're using:** their caches wait, and join by themselves as soon as you quit those apps.
- Experts can still open the full list, with every item's reason.

### Scan your whole disk
- Choose **Home Folder** or **Entire Disk** right next to the Scan button. PureClean asks you once
  to allow Macintosh HD.
- When a lot of your disk wasn't part of the last scan, Overview tells you how much and offers to
  scan everything.
- A whole-disk scan shows the same colorful maps and charts as a home-folder scan, and PureClean
  explains up front when Full Disk Access would save you a series of macOS permission questions.

### Orphaned Remnants, redesigned
- One row per deleted app, with how much it left behind and when it was last used.
- "Why PureClean thinks it's gone" explains the evidence; uncertain items are labeled *Needs Review*.
- Finds leftovers of apps that are still in the Trash, and fewer false matches for apps you still have.
- *Select All Likely* in one click; *Ignore* remembers apps you want to keep.

### Duplicates, Developer Storage and Performance
- Duplicates: a preview and Keep / Remove for every copy, Auto-Select, and a summary by kind.
- Developer Storage: every location explained, with what removing it costs and selection by age.
- Performance: live measurements with trend lines and health checks that show their evidence.

### Everywhere
- **23 languages**, including Arabic with a right-to-left interface — new: Spanish, Hindi, Vietnamese,
  Thai and Indonesian.
- New Settings, organized like System Settings, with search.
- Storage Explorer updates instantly after a cleanup or a Put Back — no rescan needed.
- Cleanup history is recorded before anything moves, so a cleanup can always be traced and undone.
- Many reliability fixes.
