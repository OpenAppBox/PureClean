# Privacy

**PureClean collects nothing.** It works entirely offline, and nothing you do in it ever leaves your
Mac.

## What PureClean does not do

- No account and no sign-in
- No analytics, telemetry, usage statistics or crash reporting
- No advertising and no advertising identifiers
- No update checks or other connections to our servers — PureClean contains no network code at all
- No reading of your files' contents (duplicate search reads a file only to compare it, on your Mac)

## What PureClean stores, and where

To show your storage and your cleanup history, PureClean keeps a small database **on your Mac only**:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| File and folder names, paths, sizes and dates from your scans | To show maps, lists and changes between scans |
| Your exclusions | So PureClean skips what you told it to skip |
| Your cleanup history | So you can see what was moved and put items back |
| Your settings | Language, appearance and scan choices |

Location: PureClean's own app container,
`~/Library/Containers/com.openappbox.PureMac/Data/Library/Application Support/com.openappbox.PureMac/`. You can delete scans and history anytime in **Settings → Privacy
& Data**, or turn off detailed history so file paths aren't recorded at all.

## The one link that opens a browser

The website link in PureClean's About window opens [openappbox.com](https://www.openappbox.com) in your
browser — only when you click it.

## Buying PureClean

If you subscribe through an app store, that store handles the payment under its own privacy policy.
OpenAppBox never sees your payment details. See the [OpenAppBox privacy policy](https://www.openappbox.com/privacy).

## Check it yourself

You don't have to take our word for any of this. PureClean's complete source code is in this
repository — anyone can read it and confirm that it makes no network connections and collects no
data.
