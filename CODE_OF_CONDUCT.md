# Code of Conduct

PureClean is made by a small team and a community of people who care about good, honest software. We
want everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in this
project — to feel welcome and respected.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** People come with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience.
- **Be constructive.** Criticize ideas and code, never people. Explain *why*, and suggest a better way.
- **Assume good intent,** and ask when something is unclear.
- **Respect people's time and privacy.** Never share someone's personal information without their permission.
- **Help newcomers.** Everyone was new once.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks
- Discriminatory jokes or language, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, deliberately derailing conversations, or repeated off-topic posts
- Publishing someone's private information
- Spam and self-promotion unrelated to the conversation

## What happens if someone breaks these rules

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock conversations, and — for serious or repeated
problems — block people from the project. We'll act fairly, consider the context, and explain our
decision where we can.

## Reporting a problem

If something happened that made you uncomfortable, write to
[support@openappbox.com](mailto:support@openappbox.com) with "Code of Conduct" in the subject. We'll
read every report, keep it confidential, and respond as soon as we can.

This code applies to everyone in the project's spaces, including the maintainers.
