# Getting help with PureClean

## Something isn't working

[Open an issue](https://github.com/OpenAppBox/PureClean/issues/new/choose) so everyone can follow the
fix. Helpful to include:

- Your PureClean version (**PureClean → About PureClean**) and macOS version
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened
- Screenshots, if you have them — please hide any file names you'd rather not share

## A question or an idea

- **Ideas and feature requests:** [open an issue](https://github.com/OpenAppBox/PureClean/issues/new/choose)
  and tell us what you're trying to get done.
- **Questions about using PureClean:** see the [README](README.md#questions),
  [How PureClean keeps your files safe](docs/safety.md) and [openappbox.com/pureclean](https://www.openappbox.com/pureclean).
- **Prefer email?** Write to [support@openappbox.com](mailto:support@openappbox.com). Every message is
  read by the people who build PureClean.

## Subscriptions and billing

Subscriptions, cancellations and refunds are handled by the store you bought from. You can manage
or cancel them anytime in your store account. For the Mac App Store, refunds can be requested at
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Security problems

Please report them privately — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Common answers

**PureClean lists some locations as "not readable".**
macOS protects some folders, such as Mail and Safari. Allow PureClean under **System Settings → Privacy
& Security → Full Disk Access** to measure them too. It's optional.

**Free space didn't go up after a cleanup.**
Items in the Trash still use space until you empty it. PureClean never empties the Trash for you.

**An app's caches were "held back".**
The app is running. Quit it (in Smart Clean, *Show* brings it to the front) and PureClean includes them by itself.

**I want something back.**
Open **History** in PureClean and choose *Put Back*, or open the Trash in Finder and choose
**File → Put Back**.
